## Cushion obstacle course
18-48 | 20 | high | indoors | Sofa cushions, pillows, a blanket | pd,cl | rainy
- Lay cushions on the floor as stepping stones.
- Add a blanket "tunnel" over two chairs.
- Show them the route: over, under, through.
- Cheer and narrate: "You went under! Now over!"
- Let them redesign the course.
why: Climbing and crawling build strength and balance, and position words come free.
easier: A line of cushions to walk along.
harder: Add rules: hop on red cushions, tiptoe on blue.
safety: Clear hard edges and stay close by.
pl: poduszka, skok (cushion, jump)
plp: Hop na poduszkę! (Hop onto the cushion!)

## Freeze dance
18-48 | 10 | high | indoors | Music (or just your voice) | pd,ead,pse | rainy
- Put on music and dance together.
- Stop the music and shout "Freeze!"
- Hold still like statues, then giggle.
- Start again. Try slow songs and fast songs.
why: Stopping and starting on cue builds self-control, an early executive skill.
easier: Dance holding hands, freeze together.
harder: Freeze in a shape: a star, a cat, a tree.
pl: tańczymy, stop (we dance, stop)
plp: Tańczymy… stop! Nie ruszaj się. (Dance… stop! Do not move.)

## Animal walks
18-48 | 10 | high | indoors,outdoors | None | pd,utw |
- Stomp like an elephant.
- Hop like a bunny or frog.
- Crawl like a bear on hands and feet.
- Tiptoe like a mouse, then slither like a snake.
why: Different movements build balance, coordination and strength.
easier: Stick to stomping and tiptoeing.
harder: Call out animals for them to switch between fast.
pl: słoń, żaba (elephant, frog)
plp: Idziemy jak słoń: tup, tup! (Walk like an elephant: stomp, stomp!)

## Lily pad hop
24-48 | 10 | high | indoors | Paper, cushions or tea towels | pd,ead |
- Put a few "lily pads" on the floor a short step apart.
- Be frogs: crouch, then jump from pad to pad.
- Say "ribbit!" on every jump.
- Spread them a little further apart.
why: Jumping with both feet is a skill most children reach around 2 and a half.
easier: Step from pad to pad, holding your hand.
harder: Count the jumps as you go.
safety: Use cushions, or tea towels on carpet only: paper and towels slide on hard floors.
pl: żabka, skacz (frog, jump)
plp: Skacz jak żabka! (Jump like a frog!)

## Follow the leader
24-48 | 10 | high | outdoors,indoors | None | pd,pse |
- You lead first: big steps, tiny steps, tiptoes.
- Walk backwards slowly, then march.
- Swap so they lead and you copy.
- Copy them exactly, even the silly bits.
why: Leading and following teaches turn-taking and body control.
easier: Hold hands and march together.
harder: Add sounds to each move.
pl: za mną (after me)
plp: Chodź za mną! (Follow me!)

## Ball roll and kick
18-36 | 10 | high | outdoors,indoors | A soft ball | pd,pse |
- Sit opposite each other, legs apart, and roll the ball back and forth.
- Count each roll.
- Stand up and try gentle kicks to each other.
- Cheer every kick, even the misses.
why: Kicking a ball is a 2-year milestone; rolling back and forth is a turn-taking game.
easier: Roll only, sitting close.
harder: Kick into a "goal" between two shoes.
pl: piłka (ball)
plp: Turlaj piłkę do mnie! (Roll the ball to me!)

## Bubble chase
18-36 | 10 | high | outdoors,bath | Washing-up liquid and water, or bubbles | pd,utw |
- Blow bubbles and let them chase and pop them.
- Say "Pop!" each time.
- Blow one big bubble slowly. Can they catch it?
- Ask where the bubbles went.
why: Tracking and reaching for bubbles builds hand-eye coordination.
easier: Blow bubbles low so they can reach.
harder: Pop only with a finger, or only with a clap.
safety: Keep bubble mix away from eyes and mouth.
pl: bańki (bubbles)
plp: Złap bańkę! (Catch a bubble!)

## Puddle walk
18-48 | 20 | high | outdoors | Wellies and waterproofs | pd,utw | rainy,autumn,winter
- Put on wellies and head out after the rain.
- Jump in puddles together. Big splash, small splash.
- Drop a leaf in a puddle and watch it float.
- Talk about wet, dry, cold, splash.
why: Rainy-day play is full of new words and big movement.
easier: Walk around the edges and stamp.
harder: Guess which puddle is deepest, then test with a stick.
safety: Keep away from roads and deep or moving water.
pl: kałuża (puddle)
plp: Hop do kałuży! Chlap! (Hop into the puddle! Splash!)

- Go on a slow walk and collect leaves.
- Find big ones, small ones, crunchy ones.

## Stick, stone, leaf
24-48 | 20 | med | outdoors | None | utw,math |
- On a walk, ask them to find a stick.
- Then a leaf, then a stone (a big one).
- Feel each: rough, smooth, crunchy, bendy.
- Make a little face on the ground with them.
why: Hunting and naming textures builds descriptive words.
easier: Find just one thing at a time.
harder: Find three of the same thing and count them.
safety: Under 3s: only stones bigger than their fist, and watch for mouthing.
pl: patyk, kamień, liść (stick, stone, leaf)
plp: Znajdź patyk! (Find a stick!)

## Seafront wind walk
18-48 | 20 | med | outdoors | Warm coat | utw,cl,pd | seaside
- Walk along the seafront promenade together.
- Notice the wind: hair blowing, waves, gulls.
- Stop and listen: what can we hear?
- Name what you see: waves, boats, birds, pebbles.
why: Talking about what you notice together is serve-and-return on the move.
easier: Do a short stretch and count gulls.
harder: Ask "Where do you think the boat is going?"
safety: Hold hands near steps, groynes and the water's edge.
pl: morze, mewa (sea, gull)
plp: Słyszysz morze? (Can you hear the sea?)

## Pebble sorting on the beach
24-48 | 20 | low | outdoors | None | math,utw | seaside
- Sit together on the pebbles.
- Find a big pebble, a small pebble, a white one, a stripy one.
- Make a line from smallest to biggest.
- Leave them on the beach when you go.
why: Sorting by size and colour is early maths, and the beach is a free maths kit.
easier: Just find "big" and "little".
harder: Find pebbles that match a colour you name.
safety: Under 3s: only pebbles bigger than their fist, and supervise mouthing. In summer, sun hat and sun cream.
pl: kamień (stone)
plp: Duży czy mały kamień? (Big or small stone?)

## Wave chasing
24-48 | 10 | high | outdoors | Wellies in cool weather | pd,utw,pse | seaside,summer
- Hold hands at the water's edge on a calm day.
- Walk towards the sea as a wave goes out.
- Run back up the beach as the next one comes in.
- Laugh and do it again.
why: Big laughter and a bit of thrill, safely shared, builds trust.
easier: Watch from further back and shout "here it comes!"
harder: Count the waves.
safety: Hold hands the whole time. Only on calm days, never alone near the sea. Sun hat and sun cream in summer.
pl: fala, morze (wave, sea)
plp: Uciekamy przed falą! (Run from the wave!)

## Sea sounds
18-48 | 5 | low | outdoors | None | utw,cl | seaside
- Sit still and close your eyes together.
- Listen: waves, gulls, wind, people.
- Open eyes and tell each other what you heard.
- Make the sounds: shhh, kee-kee, whoosh.
why: Listening games are the foundation for hearing sounds in words later.
easier: Just make the shh-shh wave sound together.
harder: Count how many different sounds you heard.
