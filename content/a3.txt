## Washing the veg
18-48 | 10 | low | kitchen | A bowl of water, some potatoes or carrots | pd,utw,pse |
- Put a bowl of water on a towel on the floor or a low table.
- Give them the veg and a clean sponge or brush.
- Scrub together and talk: muddy, clean, wet.
- Thank them for helping make dinner.
why: Real jobs build confidence, and the kitchen is full of new words.
easier: Just splash the veg in the water.
harder: Count the clean ones into a pan.
safety: Keep them away from the hob and knives.
pl: marchewka, ziemniak (carrot, potato)
plp: Myjemy marchewkę. (We are washing the carrot.)

## Mixing bowl
24-48 | 20 | med | kitchen | A simple recipe (pancakes, porridge, flapjacks) | pd,math,utw | weekend
- Pick an easy recipe with lots of tipping and stirring.
- Let them tip in the pre-measured ingredients.
- Take turns stirring and count the stirs.
- Talk about how it changes: powdery, sticky, runny.
why: Cooking is early science, maths and a lot of pride.
easier: Just stirring, with you holding the bowl.
harder: Let them crack an egg into a separate bowl.
safety: An adult handles the hob, oven and knives. No tasting raw mixture (raw egg and flour); wash hands after.
pl: miska, mieszać (bowl, stir)
plp: Mieszamy, mieszamy! (Stir, stir!)

## Pasta threading
30-48 | 20 | low | kitchen,indoors | Penne pasta and a shoelace or string | pd,math | rainy
- Tie a big knot at one end of the string.
- Show them how to thread one piece on.
- Count the pieces as they go.
- Lay it out as a long snake and admire it (no necklaces: cords round the neck aren't safe).
why: Threading takes careful two-hand control, practice for later skills like dressing.
easier: Use a pipe cleaner or a straw, which is stiffer.
harder: Make a pattern: one, then two.
