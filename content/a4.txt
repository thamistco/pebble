## Sofa picnic
18-48 | 10 | low | indoors | A blanket, a snack | pse,cl | rainy
- Spread a blanket on the floor in front of the sofa.
- Bring teddies to the picnic.
- Share a snack and serve teddy too.
- Chat about what everyone's eating.
why: A tiny change of scene turns snack time into time together, with little effort.
easier: Snack and a cuddle.
harder: Let them plan the picnic: who comes, what we eat.
safety: Sit up while eating; cut small round foods for under 3s.
pl: piknik, koc (picnic, blanket)
plp: Mamy piknik! (We are having a picnic!)

## Lie-down drawing
24-48 | 10 | low | indoors | Paper and crayons | ead,pd,cl |
- Lie on your tummies with a big sheet of paper.
- You draw a simple shape; they add to it.
- Turn a circle into a sun, a face, a ball.
- Talk about what you're making.
why: Drawing together and talking about it builds early writing and storytelling.
easier: Scribble together.
harder: Draw a picture of today and label it.

## Guess what's in the bag
24-48 | 10 | low | indoors | A pillowcase, a few familiar objects | utw,cl |
- Put three familiar things in a pillowcase.
- Let them feel inside without looking.
- Ask "Is it soft? Is it hard?"
- Guess, then pull it out.
why: Feeling and describing builds words for texture and shape.
easier: Show the objects first.
harder: Name it before pulling it out.
safety: Nothing sharp or small enough to swallow.

## Hand massage
18-48 | 5 | low | bedtime,bath | A little lotion (optional) | pse |
- Hold their hand and gently massage each finger.
- Name the fingers as you go.
- Sing "Tommy Thumb".
- Swap: they massage your hand.
why: Gentle touch is calming and a quiet way to connect at the end of a long day.
easier: Just hold hands and stroke.
harder: Count fingers, then toes.

## Lazy parent restaurant
24-48 | 10 | low | indoors | Toy food or nothing | ead,cl,pse |
- Lie on the sofa. You're the hungry customer.
- They bring you pretend food.
- Taste it with lots of drama: "Mmm, delicious spaghetti!"
- Ask for pudding.
why: You rest; they lead the play. Following their lead is the point.
easier: They bring you one thing; you eat it noisily.
harder: Order something silly like jam on your toes.

## Doctor on the sofa
24-48 | 10 | low | indoors | None | ead,pse,cl |
- Lie down and tell them you're poorly.
- Let them check your heartbeat, your ears, your knees.
- Ask "What do I need, Doctor?"

why: A low-energy game for you, a big pretend job for them.

## Weekend walk to the pier
24-48 | 30 | med | outdoors | Pushchair for when legs get tired | pd,utw,cl | seaside,weekend
- Pick a seafront walk you know.
- Let them walk as far as they can, then rest in the pushchair.
- Stop at benches and talk about what you see.
- Take one photo together and talk about it later.
why: The NHS recommends letting toddlers walk part of the way: it builds strength and shared attention.
easier: Shorter stretch, more stops.
harder: Count the beach huts or benches.
safety: Hold hands near the road and the water.

- Fill a tray with dry rice or oats.
- Bury a toy and dig it out.

## Ice rescue
30-48 | 20 | low | kitchen | Plastic toys (too big to swallow) frozen in a tub of water, warm water, a spoon | utw,pd | summer
- The night before, freeze a toy in a tub of water.
- Tip out the ice block onto a tray.
- Rescue the toy with warm water and a spoon.
- Talk about cold, melting, slippery.
why: Watching ice melt is real science, with a mission.
easier: Use a thin layer of ice.
harder: Guess how long it will take.
safety: Warm, not hot, water.

## Dance to a Polish song
18-48 | 10 | high | indoors | Your voice or music | ead,pd,cl | polish
- Choose a Polish children's song you know, like "Jedzie pociąg z daleka".
- Make a train: hold hands or hold on to hips.
- Chug around the room, stopping at "stations".
- Pick up teddies as passengers.
why: Songs in your home languages give children words in both, and the NHS encourages chatting in the languages you speak.
easier: Sway and clap along.
harder: Let them be the train driver and choose the stops.
pl: pociąg (train)

words: Jedzie pociąg z daleka, / ani chwili nie czeka. / Konduktorze łaskawy, / zabierz nas do Warszawy!

## Kosi kosi łapci clapping
18-30 | 5 | low | bedtime,indoors | None | cl,pse | polish
- Sit face to face.
- Clap hands while saying "Kosi kosi łapci", or any clapping rhyme you know.
- Clap their hands gently with yours.
- Finish with a tickle or a hug.
why: Clapping rhymes are a simple serve-and-return in any language.
easier: Just clap and smile.
harder: Clap faster, then slower.
pl: łapki, babcia (little hands, grandma)
plp: Klaszczemy! (Let's clap!)
words: Kosi, kosi łapci, / pojedziem do babci. / Babcia da nam mleczka, / a dziadek jajeczka.
en: Clap, clap little hands, we'll go to Grandma's. Grandma will give us milk, and Grandad an egg.
