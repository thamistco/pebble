## Narrator, not director
18-48 | 10 | low | indoors | Whatever they're already playing with | cl,pse |
- Sit on the floor next to them and put your phone in another room.
- Watch for a minute before you join in.
- Say out loud what they're doing: "You're putting the car in the box."
- Copy their play instead of steering it. Let them lead.
- Wait. Give them time to answer you, even with a look or a sound.
why: Following their lead and naming what they do is serve-and-return in its simplest form.
easier: Just describe. No questions.
harder: Add one new word each time: "The red car is going fast."
pl: bawimy się (we're playing)
plp: Co robisz? Jedzie autko! (What are you doing? The car is going!)

## Picture-book detectives
18-48 | 10 | low | bedtime,indoors | Any picture book | lit,cl |
- Let them choose the book, even if it's the same one again.
- Skip the words. Talk about the pictures instead.
- Ask "Where's the…?" and wait for them to point.
- Make the animal noises and pull the faces.
- Let them turn the pages.
why: Talking about pictures builds vocabulary as much as reading the text does.
easier: Point and name things yourself, slowly.
harder: Ask "What happens next?" before you turn the page.
pl: gdzie jest…? (where is…?)
plp: Gdzie jest piesek? Pokaż! (Where is the dog? Show me!)

## The same book, three ways
24-48 | 10 | low | bedtime | A favourite book | lit,cl |
- Read it once the normal way.
- Read it again in a silly voice (whisper, robot, giant).
- Third time, stop before the last word of a line and let them finish it.
- Celebrate every guess, right or wrong.
why: Repetition and rhyme help toddlers predict words, an early reading skill.
easier: Do just the silly voice.
harder: Let them "read" it to you from the pictures.
pl: książka, strona (book, page)
plp: Przewróć stronę. (Turn the page.)

## Two choices
18-48 | 5 | low | indoors,kitchen,onthego | None | cl,pse |
- At snack, dressing or bath time, hold up two options.
- Name both: "Apple or banana?" "Blue socks or stripy socks?"
- Wait for a point or a word.
- Repeat their choice back in a short sentence: "Banana! You chose banana."
why: Choices give toddlers words and a little control, which helps with big feelings.
easier: Hold the actual objects so they can point.
harder: Offer three, or ask why they picked it.
pl: to czy to? (this or this?)
plp: Jabłko czy banan? (Apple or banana?)

## Feelings faces
24-48 | 10 | low | indoors,bedtime | A mirror (optional) | pse,cl |
- Pull a happy face and say "Happy!"
- Then sad, surprised, cross, sleepy.
- Ask them to copy you, in the mirror if you have one.
- Take turns: they make a face, you guess it.
why: Naming feelings is the first step to managing them.
easier: Stick to happy and sad.
harder: Ask "What makes you feel happy?"
pl: wesoły, smutny (happy, sad)
plp: Jak się czujesz? (How do you feel?)

## Where's teddy hiding?
18-36 | 10 | med | indoors | A soft toy | utw,cl,math |
- Show them teddy, then hide him while they cover their eyes.
- Start easy: half-sticking out of a cushion.
- Give clues with position words: "He's under something."
- Swap: they hide teddy, you look (loudly and badly).
why: Hiding games build memory and position words like under, behind and in.
easier: Hide teddy while they watch.
harder: Hide him in another room and give two-step clues.
pl: pod, na, w (under, on, in)
plp: Gdzie jest miś? Pod poduszką! (Where is teddy? Under the cushion!)

## Pots and pans band
18-36 | 10 | high | kitchen,indoors | Pots, wooden spoons, a plastic tub | ead,cl,pd | rainy
- Put two or three pots upside down on the floor.
- Bang a beat and say the sound: "Bang! Tap tap!"
- Copy their rhythm back to them.
- Play loud, then whisper-quiet, then stop: freeze!
why: Noisy play builds listening skills and sound words, and it's a turn-taking game.
easier: Just bang together and cheer.
harder: Play a pattern (loud-quiet-loud) for them to copy.
pl: głośno, cicho (loud, quiet)
plp: Bum, bum! Teraz cichutko. (Boom, boom! Now very quietly.)

## Sock puppets
24-48 | 20 | low | indoors | A clean odd sock | ead,cl,pse | rainy

- Let the puppet be shy and ask your child to say hello.
- The puppet asks easy questions: "What's your name? What's that?"
- Offer them a sock so their puppet can talk to yours.
why: Children often talk more to a puppet than to a grown-up.
easier: Puppet just says hello and gives kisses.
harder: Act out a tiny story: puppet is hungry, what shall we feed it?
pl: skarpetka, buzia (sock, mouth)
plp: Cześć! Jak masz na imię? (Hi! What is your name?)

24-48 | 10 | low | indoors | Clean laundry | math,cl |
- Tip the socks onto the floor.
- Find one sock and ask them to find its twin.
- Name colours and sizes: "Daddy's big sock, your little sock."
- Count the pairs together.
why: Matching and sorting are the start of maths thinking.
easier: Sort into two piles: big and little.
harder: Sort by colour, then count each pile.
pl: duży, mały (big, small)
plp: Gdzie jest para? (Where is the pair?)

## Shadow animals
24-48 | 10 | low | bedtime | A torch or phone light | ead,utw,cl |
- Dim the room and shine the light at a wall.
- Make a dog, bird or rabbit with your hands.
- Make the animal talk or sneak up on their toes.
- Let them hold the light and move the shadows.
why: Playing with light and shadow sparks curiosity about how things work.

harder: Ask them to make shadows big or small by moving closer.

pl: cień, zajączek (shadow, bunny)
plp: Kto to jest? Zajączek! (Who is that? A bunny!)

## Kind hands
24-48 | 10 | low | indoors | A teddy or doll | pse |
- Teddy has a pretend "ouch".
- Ask how teddy feels and what would help.
- Give teddy a cuddle, a plaster or a drink together.
- Thank them for being kind.
why: Around 2, children start noticing when others are upset; practising care builds empathy.
easier: You show the cuddle; they copy.
harder: Teddy is sad because he lost his toy. What could we do?
pl: miś, boli (teddy, it hurts)
plp: Pogłaszcz misia delikatnie. (Stroke teddy gently.)
