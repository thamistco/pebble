## Idzie rak nieborak
18-48 | 5 | low | indoors,bedtime,onthego | None | cl,pse | polish
- Sit them on your lap or lie them down facing you.
- Walk two fingers slowly up their leg or arm like a little crab.
- On "czasem naprzód, czasem wspak" walk forwards, then backwards.
- On "znak!" give a gentle pinch or tickle, then wait for them to ask for more.
words: Idzie rak nieborak, / czasem naprzód, czasem wspak. / Idzie rak nieborak, / jak uszczypnie, będzie znak!
en: Here comes a poor little crab, sometimes forwards, sometimes back. If he pinches, he'll leave a mark!
why: The pause before the tickle builds anticipation, and toddlers soon start saying "znak!" themselves.
easier: Just the short version: "Idzie rak nieborak, jak uszczypnie, będzie znak!"
harder: Swap roles: let them walk their fingers up your arm and say the words.
pl: Jeszcze raz? (Again?)

## Sroczka kaszkę warzyła
18-48 | 5 | low | indoors,bedtime,onthego | None | cl,pse,math | polish
- Hold their hand palm up and stir little circles in it with one finger.
- On each "temu dała" hold or wiggle one finger, starting from the little finger.
- On "frrr!" fly your hand away like a bird, or up to tickle under their arm.
- Do the other hand too.
words: Sroczka kaszkę warzyła, / dzieci swoje karmiła. / Temu dała na łyżeczce, / temu dała na miseczce, / temu dała na spodeczku, / temu dała w garnuszeczku, / a dla tego nic nie miała, / frrr! po więcej poleciała.
en: The magpie cooked some porridge and fed her babies: this one on a spoon, this one in a bowl, this one on a saucer, this one in a pot, and for this one there was none left, so frrr! she flew off for more.
why: Touching each finger in turn builds body awareness and early counting, and the fly-away is pure joy.
easier: Just stir the palm and do "frrr!"
harder: Let them be the magpie on your hand.
pl: paluszek, rączka (little finger, little hand)

## Wspinał się pajączek
18-48 | 5 | low | bedtime,indoors | None | pse,cl | polish
- Sit them in front of you with their back to you.
- Walk your fingers up their back: the spider climbs the drainpipe.
- Rain falls: sweep both flat hands down their back.
- The sun comes out: rub slow warm circles. Then start again.
words: Wspinał się pajączek po rynnie. / Spadł wielki deszcz i zmył pajączka. / Zaświeciło słoneczko, / wysuszyło pajączka, rynnę i… / wspinał się pajączek po rynnie…
en: A little spider climbed up the drainpipe. Down came the rain and washed the spider out. Out came the sun and dried the spider and the drainpipe, and the spider climbed again. (The Polish Incy Wincy.)
why: A gentle back massage rhyme calms a busy body and is a lovely wind-down before bed.
easier: Do it on their tummy while they lie down.
harder: Let them do it on your back while you say the words.
pl: pajączek, deszcz, słoneczko (spider, rain, sun)

## Wlazł kotek na płotek
18-48 | 5 | low | indoors,onthego,bedtime | None | cl,ead | polish
- Sing it slowly, blinking your eyes on "mruga".
- Climb two fingers up their arm like a kitten on a fence.
- On "jeszcze raz" (once more), sing it again, faster or as a whisper.
words: Wlazł kotek na płotek i mruga, / ładna to piosenka, niedługa. / Niedługa, nie krótka, lecz w sam raz, / zaśpiewaj, koteczku, jeszcze raz.
en: A kitten climbed the fence and blinks. It's a pretty song, not long. Not long, not short, but just right. Sing it, kitten, once more.
why: A short, repetitive tune with an action is perfect for joining in with first words.
easier: Just blink and sing the first line.
harder: Pause and let them fill in "mruga" or "jeszcze raz".
pl: kotek mruga (the kitten blinks)

## Jedzie, jedzie pan
18-48 | 5 | med | indoors | A chair | pd,cl | polish
- Sit on a chair and sit them on your knees facing you, holding their hands or under their arms.
- Bounce gently for the gentleman: slow and grand.
- Bounce faster for the farmer: "hop, hop!"
- On "z konika spada" open your knees a little and let them dip safely between, holding them tight.
words: Jedzie, jedzie pan, pan, / na koniku sam, sam. / Za nim jedzie chłop, chłop, / na koniku hop, hop! / Jedzie, jedzie baba / i z konika spada!
en: Here rides a gentleman on his horse all alone. Behind him a farmer, hop hop! Here rides a granny, and she falls off the horse!
why: Knee-bouncing rhymes build balance and core strength, and the predictable "fall" is thrilling and safe.
easier: Just gentle bouncing, no drop.
harder: Ask "Jeszcze raz?" and wait for a word or sign before you go again.
safety: Hold them firmly the whole time; keep the "fall" small.
pl: hop, hop! (hop, hop!)

## Ślimak, ślimak, pokaż rogi
18-48 | 5 | low | outdoors,indoors | None | cl,utw | polish
- Make snail horns with two fingers on your head and say the rhyme.
- Let them copy the horns.
- If you're outside after rain, look for a real snail together (look, don't pick up).
words: Ślimak, ślimak, pokaż rogi, / dam ci sera na pierogi. / Jak nie sera, to kapusty, / od kapusty będziesz tłusty.
en: Snail, snail, show me your horns, I'll give you cheese for dumplings. If not cheese, then cabbage, and cabbage will make you fat.
why: A silly rhyme plus a real-world find connects words with things they can see.
easier: Just "Ślimak, ślimak, pokaż rogi!" with the horns.
harder: Talk about where snails live and why they come out in the rain.
pl: Gdzie jest ślimak? (Where's the snail?)

## Stary niedźwiedź mocno śpi
24-48 | 10 | high | indoors,outdoors | None | pd,pse,cl | polish,rainy
- You're the bear first: curl up on the floor and pretend to sleep.
- They tiptoe round you while you both sing.
- After "trzecia godzina niedźwiedź łapie!" wake up and slowly "chase" them.
- Swap: now they're the bear.
words: Stary niedźwiedź mocno śpi, / stary niedźwiedź mocno śpi. / My się go boimy, / na palcach chodzimy, / jak się zbudzi, to nas zje, / jak się zbudzi, to nas zje. / Pierwsza godzina – niedźwiedź śpi, / druga godzina – niedźwiedź chrapie, / trzecia godzina – niedźwiedź łapie!
en: The old bear is fast asleep. We're scared of him, we tiptoe round. If he wakes up he'll eat us! One o'clock, the bear's asleep; two o'clock, the bear snores; three o'clock, the bear catches!
why: Tiptoeing, waiting and running is great for self-control and big movement.
easier: Leave out the chase; just a big roar and a cuddle.
harder: Play with more family, in a circle.
pl: Cicho! Niedźwiedź śpi. (Shh! The bear is sleeping.)

## Panie Janie
18-48 | 5 | low | bedtime,indoors | None | cl,ead | polish
- Sing it as a wake-up song in the morning, or slowly at bedtime.
- Make a big stretch on "rano wstań" (get up in the morning).
- Swing your arms like bells on "bim, bam, bom".
words: Panie Janie, panie Janie, / rano wstań, rano wstań! / Wszystkie dzwony biją, wszystkie dzwony biją, / bim, bam, bom, bim, bam, bom.
en: Mister John, Mister John, get up in the morning! All the bells are ringing, bim bam bom. (Frère Jacques.)
why: The same tune in Polish, English or French shows children that languages can share a song.
easier: Just "bim, bam, bom" with swinging arms.
harder: Sing it as a round with another grown-up.
pl: Dzień dobry! Wstajemy! (Good morning! Time to get up!)

## Kółko graniaste
18-48 | 5 | med | indoors,outdoors | None | pd,pse | polish,rainy
- Hold both hands and walk round in a little circle while you sing.
- On "a my wszyscy bęc!" sit down on the floor together with a bump.
- Get up and go again, faster or slower.
words: Kółko graniaste, / czworokanciaste, / kółko nam się połamało, / cztery grosze kosztowało, / a my wszyscy bęc!
en: A round ring with corners, four-cornered. Our ring broke; it cost four pennies. And we all fall down, bump! (Like Ring a Ring o' Roses.)

easier: Sway on the spot instead of walking round.
harder: Add a teddy or more family to the circle.
pl: Bęc! (Bump!)

- Walk round with the "handkerchief" while you all sing.

en: Head, shoulders, knees and heels… eyes, ears, mouth, nose. (The Polish Head, Shoulders, Knees and Toes.)

- Make a buzzing fly with your fingers and circle it round them.

- Buzz louder and softer to keep them guessing.
words: Lata mucha koło ucha, / lata bąk koło rąk, / lecą ważki koło paszki, / lata pszczoła koło czoła, / lata mucha koło brzucha.
en: A fly buzzes round your ear, a bumblebee round your hands, dragonflies round your armpit, a bee round your forehead, a fly round your tummy.
